DevSuhbat — Maxfiylik siyosati

Kuchga kirgan sana: 2026-yil 4-oktabr

DevSuhbat — dasturchilar uchun texnik intervyuga tayyorlanish ilovasi. Ushbu siyosat ilova qanday ma'lumot bilan ishlashini tushuntiradi.

Biz ma'lumot yig'maymiz

Ilova hech qanday shaxsiy ma'lumotni yig'maydi, saqlamaydi va hech kimga yubormaydi. Ro'yxatdan o'tish, akkaunt, reklama, analitika yoki uchinchi tomon SDK'lari yo'q. Ilovada internet ruxsati yo'q, shuning uchun u tarmoq orqali ma'lumot yubora olmaydi.

Qurilmada saqlanadigan ma'lumotlar

  • Tanlangan yo'nalish, daraja va ilova sozlamalari (mavzu, eslatma vaqti).
  • Javoblar tarixi va o'rganish progressi (qaysi savollar takrorlanishi kerakligi).

Bu ma'lumotlar faqat sizning qurilmangizda saqlanadi. Ularni Sozlamalardagi "Progressni tozalash" orqali yoki ilovani o'chirib yo'q qilishingiz mumkin.

Eksport va import

"Progressni eksport qilish" progressingizni JSON faylga yozadi. Faylni qayerga saqlashni o'zingiz tanlaysiz; ilova uni hech qayerga yubormaydi. Import faqat siz tanlagan faylni o'qiydi.

Bildirishnomalar

Kunlik eslatma ixtiyoriy va default holatda o'chiq. Uni yoqsangiz, ilova bildirishnoma ruxsatini so'raydi va tanlangan vaqtda qurilmaning o'zida eslatma chiqaradi. Eslatmani rejalashtirish uchun tizim xizmatlari (qurilma qayta yoqilganda eslatmani tiklash) ishlatiladi; bu ma'lumot yig'ish emas.

Xato haqida xabar

"Xato haqida xabar berish" tugmasi savol matni va ilova versiyasi yozilgan xabarni tizimning ulashish oynasida ochadi. Xabarni yuborish yoki yubormaslikni, qaysi ilova orqali yuborishni o'zingiz hal qilasiz.

Bolalar

Ilova bolalarga mo'ljallanmagan va bolalardan ma'lumot yig'maydi.

O'zgarishlar

Siyosat o'zgarsa, ushbu sahifa yangilanadi va yuqoridagi sana o'zgaradi.

DevSuhbat — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

DevSuhbat is an app that helps developers prepare for technical interviews. This policy explains how the app handles data.

We do not collect data

The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. There is no sign-up, account, advertising, analytics or third-party SDK. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send data over the network.

Data stored on your device

  • Your chosen field, level and app settings (theme, reminder time).
  • Your answer history and learning progress (which questions are due for review).

This data stays on your device only. You can delete it with "Reset progress" in Settings or by uninstalling the app.

Export and import

"Export progress" writes your progress to a JSON file at a location you choose; the app does not send it anywhere. Import reads only the file you pick.

Notifications

The daily reminder is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, the app asks for the notification permission and shows a reminder on the device at the time you choose. System scheduling services (for example, restoring the reminder after a reboot) are used for this; they do not collect data.

Reporting a mistake

The "Report a mistake" button opens the system share sheet with the question text and the app version. You decide whether to send it and through which app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
